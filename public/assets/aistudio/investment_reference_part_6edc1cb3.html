Account Terms & Requirements

Profit Sharing
50% of the profits are retained by the company, Lucky Mane Group Ltd.
The remaining 50% is credited to the client

Loss Coverage
Accounts funded with equity above $5,000 will be eligible for a 10% refund of their deposited amount

Management Period
The management contract is reviewed monthly or quarterly, depending on the agreed timeline.
Access to Portfolio
The client receives regular updates and can monitor account performance as agreed upon.

Withdrawal Policy
Withdrawal terms, including any applicable processing fees and timelines, will be outlined in the contract or agreed upon through direct discussion with Lucky Mane Group Ltd.
Client’s Role
The client ensures timely equity deposits and maintains clear communication with the assigned account manager. The client must not trade on the account during this period

Customization
The investment portfolio is tailored based on client preferences, risk appetite, and market conditions.
Equity Deposit
The specified equity amount for this phase is required to open and actively manage your account
The client should fund their account and send logins. The capital remains the clients own, and we do not have access to withdraw from account.

Client Vetting
A KYC form must be completed, followed by a video call to verify identity, assess investment goals, and confirm understanding of the terms.

Subscription Fee
A subscription fee is required to be able to gain access to services available.
Profit Target
Profit targets are determined based on the selected plan, investment strategy, and risk profile — typically reaching or exceeding it. It can go up to $10,000, though some plans may have lower benchmarks.
